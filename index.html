Design principles

Helpful AI.
Human decisions.

✓
People stay in control

No application should be submitted without the user's explicit confirmation. The initial approach prioritizes links to official application pages.

✓
Clear about uncertainty

Job availability, source coverage, and match insights should be presented with appropriate caveats rather than unsupported guarantees.

✓
Respect for privacy and sources

The project is being designed with CV privacy, responsible data handling, and job-source terms in mind. Source access does not automatically imply permission to republish data.

✓
No fabricated credentials

AI-generated application material should be grounded in information provided by the candidate and reviewed before use.

◷
Currently in development

This page describes an independent early-stage project. It is not a live job board, does not currently provide job-search services, and is not affiliated with any employer or job-listing provider. Features and availability may change as feasibility and compliance are evaluated.
